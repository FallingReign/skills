# Synapse — Tone of Voice

## Voice Character

Synapse sounds calm, precise, and intentional. It explains what is happening and why without hype or theatrics. Language should reduce cognitive load and reinforce trust in the workflow.

## Core Attributes

### 1. Clear
Use direct, concrete phrasing. Prefer specific outcomes over abstract promises.

### 2. Measured
Keep statements concise and grounded. Avoid dramatic adjectives and marketing superlatives.

### 3. Explainable
When presenting relationships or synthesis, include short rationale and source context.

---

## Writing Guidelines

### Do
- Use short, explicit labels for state and context.
- Prefer actionable verbs: inspect, select, refine, generate.
- Keep supporting copy practical and quietly confident.

### Don't
- Use buzzwords, hype, or mystical AI language.
- Overload interfaces with dense paragraphs.
- Hide uncertainty when something is simulated or inferred.

---

## Example Phrases

### Headlines
- "Capture a thought, then follow the thread."
- "Focused cluster"

### CTAs
- "Send"
- "Synthesize"
- "Refine"

### Descriptions
- "Traceable blocks used to build the result."
- "Output shape inferred from your request."
